Installing

Ripe is free and isn't distributed through the App Store, so macOS asks you to approve it the first time.

  1. Download Ripe.zip and double-click it to unzip.
  2. Drag Ripe into your Applications folder, then open it.
  3. When macOS says it can't verify Ripe, click Done. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Ripe. You only do this once.
  4. Look for the 🍅 in your menu bar. To move it, hold ⌘ and drag it.

Prefer Terminal? xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Ripe.app skips step 3. Or build it from source.
